sign of the verdict tracks the sign of the summary, that nothing degenerates — and it **cannot show
the verdicts are correct**, because correctness requires a label the data does not have.
**Labelled real segregation validation is deferred to Phase 16's blind evaluation, which is
precisely what the sealed holdout exists for, and precisely why Phase 13 does not open it.**

measured sits at positive induced mu. Note that the alpha series produces the first negative induced
mu obtained from real pixels, which does not refute that finding but sharpens it — negative mu is
*constructible* from real images but has not been observed to *occur* naturally in the images this
project holds.

unmeasurable from the file alone, so there is no correct value to plug in. The pre-registered
choice is to report the whole sweep across the Phase-11 ladder and headline the widest, most
conservative rung, because a conservative lambda makes the evidence *weaker*, so an exclusion
verdict that survives it is the credible one. A real image is never read at an invented single
lambda.

`[-0.44, +0.33]` — a densely-covered interior region — so it probes the sign transition and the
near-exclusion regime and **does not probe the deep-exclusion tail** near the +/-0.99 clamp atoms,
where the prior-atom asymmetry (about 2.5 to 1 against the exclusion end) lands hardest.
