<objective>
Run the pre-registered resolution probe once against the post-Phase-11 simulator, and freeze the
measured tau in a Tier-2 block.

Purpose: tau is the only number in this phase that is legitimately derived from a run, and the order
of operations is what makes it legitimate. The specification was committed with tau absent (plan
13-01); this plan runs the probe and appends the result. Any other order — running first, or editing
the Tier-1 block — makes tau a chosen number rather than a measured one.

Output: `spike/p13/run_tau_probe.jl`, the persisted curve artifact, the Tier-2 block in
`spike/p13/consts.jl`, and the updated pre-registration assertions.

**EXECUTION IS BLOCKED ON THE PHASE-11 SIMULATOR MERGE** — but NOT on Phase 11's trained net. The
probe needs `sample_prior`, `simulate_pair`, `patch_summary` and `encode_d01` only.
</objective>

<phase_11_precondition>
This plan needs the Phase-11 **simulator surgery** only: the chromatic epsilon as an eighth theta
field, the single composed affine warp in stage 6, and the widened shift prior. It does NOT need the
Phase-11 trained NPE, no `zt`, and no conditioning encoder — the probe stops before
`standardize_summary`.

Running the probe against the pre-Phase-11 simulator would measure resolution in an easier world
than the net will train in, and freezing that tau would be a pre-registration error. The runner must
therefore refuse to write a Tier-2 value when `simulator_provenance_guard()` reports the simulator
is pre-Phase-11.
</phase_11_precondition>
