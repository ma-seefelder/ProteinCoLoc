<objective>
Generate the labelled, class-stratified, conditioned training pool on the Phase-11 basis, and train
the three-way evidence net once.

Purpose: this is where the phase's three most consequential decisions meet real data — the inherited
frozen standardizer (D-02), the conditioning input appended in exactly the right place (D-03), and
class-frequency stratification with a measured per-head correction (D-07). Each has a silent failure
mode, and each is cheap to assert.

Output: `spike/p13/datagen.jl`, `spike/p13/train_three_way.jl`, the trained artifact, its test, and
the harness include.
</objective>

the research NPE is absent. There is no fallback to the shipped grid-8 basis. `P13_TAU` must also
exist (plan 13-10's Tier-2 commit) — labelling before tau is measured violates D-06, and
`p13_tau()` enforces that by erroring.
</phase_11_precondition>

**Binding rulings this plan implements (do not reopen):**
- **Class-frequency stratification only (D-07-i).** Whole classes are accepted or rejected to hit
  `P13_TARGET_CLASS_FREQ`; the within-class theta distribution is left exactly as pi gives it. Do
  NOT stratify rho within the exclusion range — that is a within-class reshape which the F2 theorem
  and the plan-13-07 negative control prove a scalar correction cannot repair. The
  importance-weighted design is the pre-declared fallback, spendable only on the D-04 trigger.
- **One lambda per pair,** appended once, after `pair_encode`.
- **The recipe is copied, not chosen.** `P13_TRAIN_N`, `P13_EPOCHS`, `P13_BATCHSIZE`, `P13_LR`,
  `P13_WEIGHT_DECAY`, `P13_VAL_FRAC`, `P13_STOPPING_EPOCHS` come from the consts file, which copied
  them from `src/amortized/train_ratio.jl:155-163`.
- **One training run.** A second run with different hyperparameters would spend the D-04 iteration
  allowance, which is reserved for the stratification switch. If the run fails to converge, that is
  a finding for the report.
- **No new dependency, no GPU.** CPU-only is the reproducible baseline and CUDA is asserted absent.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
