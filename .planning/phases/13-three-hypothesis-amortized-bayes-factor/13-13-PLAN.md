<objective>
Run the alpha-graded random-to-exclusion series through the trained net and report the curves and
the crossing point.

Purpose: the simulator's "exclusion" is negative intensity correlation across patches; a biologist's
"mutually exclusive" is disjoint spatial localization. These coincide often but not necessarily, and
this series is the only evidence in the phase that probes the difference — the corpus has exactly one
real segregated anchor and no graded series at all. The interesting output is not "does exclusion get
detected" but **at what alpha the intensity-correlation notion starts to track disjoint
localization**.

Output: `spike/p13/run_alpha_series.jl`, the reported artifact, and the figure. This is supporting
evidence: there is no gate here.
</objective>
