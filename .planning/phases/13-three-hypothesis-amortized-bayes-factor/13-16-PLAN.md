microscopy TIFFs, across the pre-registered lambda sweep, each number printed beside its
out-of-distribution verdict.

Purpose: this is the phase's only contact with real microscopy, and the most honest thing it will
produce is not a log-BF — it is the OOD flag. The shipped detector scores these fixtures at

ground-truth label, so this check can show the three-way verdicts behave sensibly on real
microscopy; it cannot show they are correct. Labelled real segregation validation is deferred to
Phase 16's blind evaluation, which is exactly what the sealed holdout exists for and exactly why
Phase 13 does not open it.

here and no threshold anywhere in the arm.
