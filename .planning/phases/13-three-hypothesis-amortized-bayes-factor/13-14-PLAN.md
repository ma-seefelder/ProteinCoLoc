<objective>
Write the Phase-13 report: what was claimed, what was measured, what was declined, and what is owed
to a later phase.

Purpose: this phase amended a success criterion, promoted a hypothesis to first class on a
research-lane net, and produced one number (`alpha_star`) that speaks to a conceptual gap nobody has
measured before. Each of those is only worth something if the write-up is as honest about the limits
as the gate was about the thresholds. The project's established mechanism for that is the named-limit
list, and Phase 13 owes it **four** entries.

The amended D-15 adds a structural requirement to this report that is easy to satisfy in the wrong
place. Phase 13 produces evidence at **three different standings** — a gate, a supporting
construction, and a qualitative real-microscopy check with no ground-truth label — and the weakest
arm is the one a reader will find most interesting. The scope-of-evidence framing therefore belongs
in the **header, before any result of any kind**, not buried in the section that reports the real
images. That placement is the same declare-it-up-front discipline D-12 applies to the SC2 amendment,
and it is what stops a qualitative reading from being quoted later as a validation.

Output: `.planning/phases/13-three-hypothesis-amortized-bayes-factor/13-REPORT.md` and the ROADMAP
checklist completion.
</objective>
