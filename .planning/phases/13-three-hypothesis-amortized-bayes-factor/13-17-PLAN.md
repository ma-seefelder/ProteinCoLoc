<objective>
Correct the real-image channel pair of the D-15 arm from the DAPI counterstain to the green/red
target pair, drop the redundancy arm, and re-run 13-16 on the corrected substrate.

Purpose: `13-01-PLAN.md` pre-registered the real arm on `P13_REAL_CHANNEL_PAIR = (1, 2)`, and
`test/runtests.jl:105` records the fixture channels as `["blue", "green", "red"]` — so `c1` is the
DAPI/Hoechst nuclear counterstain and the arm as executed measured counterstain-versus-protein
overlap, which is not colocalization. The upstream calibration source had **already** been corrected
to the c2/c3 pair on 2026-07-25 (`78dc37f`), 39 hours before `consts.jl` was written. This plan
repairs that drift under the amendment `13-D15-AMENDMENT.md` and measures the corrected arm.

**This plan buys no evidence.** Unmasked, the corrected fixtures separate by 0.0788 against 0.0811
before — marginally *worse*. It deletes an arm rather than adding one. The expected outcome is that
the conclusion does not change, and the plan requires that to be stated plainly if so, because a
limitation that survives its own correction is a stronger statement than one never tested.

Output: the amended constants, the corrected runner, a re-run `realimage_report.jld2`, updated
tests, revised real-image sections of `13-REPORT.md`, and superseding notes in `13-15-SUMMARY.md`
and `13-16-SUMMARY.md`.
</objective>
