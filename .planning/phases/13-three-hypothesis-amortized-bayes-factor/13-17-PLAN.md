**CHANGE A — the channel pair.** `13-01-PLAN.md` pre-registered the real arm on
`P13_REAL_CHANNEL_PAIR = (1, 2)`, and `test/runtests.jl:105` records the fixture channels as
`["blue", "green", "red"]` — so `c1` is the DAPI/Hoechst nuclear counterstain and the arm as

named the wrong physical object.** The upstream calibration source had **already** been corrected to
the c2/c3 pair on 2026-07-25 (`78dc37f`), **39 h 15 min** before `consts.jl` was written, and the
blocker naming Phase 13 specifically (`ea4a7d3`) landed **38 h 46 min 18 s** before it.

**CHANGE B — the include-guard sentinel.** `spike/p13/consts.jl:100` guards its entire Tier-1 body
on `:P13_DEV_SEED`, and `spike/validation/p12_consts.jl:109` **legitimately mirrors** that name in
order to assert seed disjointness. **That mirror is correct and `p12_consts.jl` must not be
touched.** In a full-suite run the wrapper sees the mirror, skips its own body, ~114 Tier-1
constants go undefined, and `runtests.jl:220` reports 114 `UndefVarError`s and aborts the eight
remaining Phase-13 test files. This is DEF-12-03, and it is one of **29** poisoned guards a
mechanical sweep found across `spike/`.

**Why the two ride together, and why that is not a smuggled second change.** The byte-lock breaks
either way: CHANGE A alone changes `consts.jl`'s sha256 and forces `h.consts_sha ==
p13_consts_sha()` to be re-derived in the gate, alpha and real-image runners. **CHANGE B therefore
rides along at ZERO additional pre-registration cost**, and holding it back would mean opening the
byte-locked file a second time later under a second amendment — strictly worse. That coupling is
exactly why the Task-1 mechanism question resolved to M1 and not M2. **Both changes are disclosed
separately, with two distinct justifications, in `13-D15-AMENDMENT.md` §0.4.**

**This plan buys no evidence.** Unmasked, the corrected fixtures separate by 0.0788 against 0.0811
before — marginally *worse*. It deletes an arm rather than adding one, and CHANGE B makes the suite
report **more** failures, not fewer. The expected outcome is that the conclusion does not change, and
the plan requires that to be stated plainly if so, because a limitation that survives its own
correction is a stronger statement than one never tested.

Output: the amended constants, a repaired and order-independent load chain, a re-derived and
strictly stronger `consts_sha` guard, the corrected runner, a re-run `realimage_report.jld2`,
updated tests, revised real-image sections **and a corrected §13** of `13-REPORT.md`, and
