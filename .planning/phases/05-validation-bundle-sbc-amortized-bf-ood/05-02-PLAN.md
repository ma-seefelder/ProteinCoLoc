<objective>
Deliver the amortized Bayes factor. Train a `RatioEstimator` as a neural model-comparison / Evidence Network over a binary model index m∈{0,1} (coloc = Δρ>0, null = Δρ≤0), then read the amortized log-BF in ONE forward pass as `logratio(Z;m=1) − logratio(Z;m=0) − log_prior_odds` — no quadgk/KDE/shuffle on the amortized side. Prove it reproduces the existing KDE `compute_BayesFactor()` over a held-out Δρ sweep within pre-registered correlation and log-BF-error tolerances.

Purpose: BF-01/02 give the paper an amortized, calibration-consistent Bayes factor that replaces the fragile KDE+quadgk baseline while provably reproducing it in the well-specified regime.

CRITICAL HONESTY NOTE: The pinned NeuralEstimators v0.2.1 has NO l-POP / Evidence-Networks loss — `RatioEstimator`'s loss is hard-coded `logitbinarycrossentropy` and a custom loss is silently ignored. The requirement's "l-POP-style loss" is therefore re-labeled honestly as the standard Hermans-2020 NRE used as a model-comparison reduction; literal l-POP is documented as a fallback only. This equals the baseline BF by construction (proof in 05-RESEARCH.md §Amortized Bayes Factor).

Output: `spike/validation/train_ratio.jl` (ratio-net training + measured log_prior_odds), `spike/validation/bf.jl` (amortized log-BF + reproduction gate), filled `spike/test/test_bf.jl`.
</objective>
