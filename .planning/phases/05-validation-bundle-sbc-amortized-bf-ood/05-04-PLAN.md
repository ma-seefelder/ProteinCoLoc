<objective>
Produce the actual publishable trifecta artifacts — not just the capability. Run the SBC calibration proof, the amortized-BF reproduction, and the OOD ROC experiment at the LOCKED pre-registered constants on a fresh, never-tuned-against, independently-seeded stream, each owned by a run script that persists its artifact and figures and hard-gates on the pre-registered thresholds.

Purpose: The phase requirements (SBC-03 "the reported SBC is a fresh held-out run") and the ROADMAP Phase-5 success criteria are present-tense factual claims — they demand completed proofs, not just tested machinery. This mirrors the Phase-4 precedent (04-05 ran the actual >100× ADVI benchmark and gated on it). Plans 01/02/03 built and fixture-tested the machinery; this Wave-3 plan runs it for real and owns the trifecta.

Anti-snooping: every threshold was committed in `spike/validation/consts.jl` (Plan 01, Task 1) BEFORE any run. The reported runs use `VAL_MASTER_SEED` (disjoint from the fixture stream `VAL_FIX_SEED` and the training stream `NPE_MASTER_SEED`). A genuine threshold failure is an HONEST negative result surfaced to the Phase-6 Go/No-Go memo — it is NEVER tuned away by editing consts.jl after the run.

Output: `spike/validation/run_sbc.jl`, `spike/validation/run_bf.jl`, `spike/validation/run_ood.jl` + the persisted `sbc_report.jld2` / `bf_report.jld2` / `ood_report.jld2` and CairoMakie figures under `spike/validation/`.
