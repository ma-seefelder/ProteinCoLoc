## RESOLVED 2026-07-28 — Δρ SEMANTICS: DELIVER ALL THREE MAPS

**This was a blocker; it is now the user's decision and this plan may execute.** The question was that in
this milestone `Δρ` means sample minus control — `src/amortized/infer.jl:107` defines
`delta_rho(est, Zsample, Zcontrol, θzt)` as "the Monte-Carlo difference of TWO INDEPENDENT single-stack
passes (D-03)", `local_map.jl:48` documents `LOCAL_MAP_SENTINEL` as "no evidence of a **sample-vs-control**
difference", and `:30` names `SpatialColocResult` / `delta_rho_map` as that map's calibrated successor —
while no Phase-12 plan built a contrast at all.

**DECISION: ship the sample map, the control map AND the difference.** The user's reasoning: a reader
benefits from seeing where a difference *comes from* rather than being handed a contrast they cannot
interrogate.

Six consequences, all binding on this document and on the plans named:

1. **Δρ remains the PRIMARY named deliverable.** `region_delta_rho` per `src/results.jl:181`, computed as
   the per-region Monte-Carlo difference mirroring `infer.jl:108`. The two single-stack maps are
   *additional exposed artifacts*, never a replacement. The package keeps exactly one meaning of Δρ.

2. **The read cost is zero extra forward passes, and datagen is unchanged — state both in §5.** A paired
   Δρ already performs both single-stack reads internally (`infer.jl:108` is
   `mean(ρ_sample_draws .- ρ_control_draws)`), so exposing the two maps adds **zero forward passes at read
   time**. And the training pool stays **single-stack**: the net maps one summary to θ and the pairing
   happens at scoring time via `harness.jl:124-136`, so datagen does not double either. Say this
   explicitly, because a paired pool would have pushed the measured 65-80 min / 50k to ~130-160 min
   against an append-only `P12_DATAGEN_WALLCLOCK_CEILING_MIN = 150`.

3. **The per-region Δρ\* ground truth comes from `harness.jl:124-136` at SCORING time, not from the pool.**
   12-16's parameter-coverage cross-check and 12-18's SBC obtain it by drawing two independent prior
   samples and differencing their fields at read time — exactly as `draw_simulate_infer_paired` already
   does. **12-09 does NOT gain a paired control field draw**, and an earlier draft of this clause that
   said it did is withdrawn here rather than deleted: it contradicted clause 2 above, and freezing both
   into `12-SC3-AMENDMENT.md` would have made a permanent, citable self-contradiction. 12-09's own
   acceptance criteria forbid any `_control` pool field.

4. **12-12's range guard is REPLACED, not tightened.** The old `[-2, 2]` bound admitted ρ ∈ [-1, 1]
   silently — the one check that could have caught the mislabelling was what hid it. Tightening cannot fix
   it either: with a TRUE Δρ the range genuinely **is** [-2, 2], so a range check simply cannot distinguish
   the two quantities. The check that can is a **structural identity**: assert the result carries all three
   maps and that `region_delta_rho ≈ region_rho_sample - region_rho_control` elementwise. That is
   unfalsifiable-by-accident in a way no range ever is, and it becomes available *precisely because* all
   three maps are now shipped.

5. **Sentinel semantics are defined PER MAP.** `local_map.jl:48`'s "no evidence of a sample-vs-control
   difference" is meaningful for the difference and meaningless for a single-stack map. §5 must state what
   a sentinel means in each of the three, and preserve the invariant in all three: a sentinel is never
   silently indistinguishable from a measured zero, and every sentinel region carries a forced OOD flag.

6. **This clause goes in the frozen amendment as §5**, which is the section this document was waiting on.

**A CONSEQUENCE FOR §4(f) THAT MUST BE WRITTEN IN, because Δρ needs a control image and real data may not
supply one.** Counted rather than assumed (see the corpus audit recorded in this plan's summary):
`corpus/data/` is **empty** and all 32 rows have `bytes = 0` and no fetched content; the two `physical-primary` rows carry `sha256 = PENDING-FETCH` and `split = sealed_holdout`, while the 30 `simulated-secondary` rows carry an EMPTY `sha256` and `split ∈ {eval, dev}`, so the Phase-8 corpus supplies **zero** images today. Its only two `physical-primary` rows are `sealed_holdout`
(Phase-16, untouchable here) and its 30 CBS rows are `simulated-secondary`, which cannot support a "real
image" claim at any n. The corpus `role` column is `positive` / `negative` / `benchmark` — those are
*experimental* positive and negative controls, i.e. **different specimens with opposite ground truth**,
not sample/control pairs of the same specimen.

So the real-image data is, and stays, the six committed TIFFs = **two specimens** (six FILES, two
DATASETS — §4(f) must use those words, because the earlier "six committed TIFFs" phrasing invited exactly
this confusion).

**§5 MUST ALSO SETTLE WHAT Δρ IS ON THE REAL ARM, AND THE HONEST ANSWER IS THAT IT IS NOT COMPUTED THERE.**
Two facts settle it:

1. **The SC3 criterion does not need a control.** D-09's leave-region-out construction masks region r in
   ONE image's summary and scores that image's own observed entry at r. Every step is single-stack. So
   predictive coverage is computable on each specimen independently: **n = 2 images, ~64 regions each.**
   §5 must add, in the same breath, that the **effective independent n is 2, not 128** — the region-draws
   are pseudo-replicates, because 12-01:172-178 pre-registers the dataset as the independent unit
   ("the 64 regions of one image share every nuisance and the global term"; counting region-draws as
   independent would overstate power "by up to 8× in sd terms"). Any interval on the real-arm coverage
   comes from the between-image spread, never from a binomial over 128.
2. **Δρ needs two EXCHANGEABLE draws, and the two specimens are not exchangeable.** State the argument in
   these terms and not in terms of shared nuisances — the simulated pair does **not** share nuisances:
   `harness.jl:124-136` draws two independent priors, exactly as the shipped Δρ does (`infer.jl:108`).
   What the simulated pair has is exchangeability: both draws come from the same π(θ), so Δρ* is a
   **within-population** difference and that is what the net is calibrated against. The two anchors are
   `role = positive` / `truth = coloc` and `role = negative` / `truth = segregated` — different specimen
   types, deliberately built to differ. Their contrast is **between-population**. Scoring a
   between-population contrast against a gate calibrated on a within-population one is two arms measuring
   different quantities under one gate — the same defect class as the Δρ mislabelling this section fixes.

So §5 states, as a **named limit** rather than an omission: the per-region Δρ map is validated on
simulation, where the sample and control are two EXCHANGEABLE draws from one prior so Δρ\* is a
within-population difference; on real data the phase demonstrates per-region predictive calibration on two
specimens, and **no exchangeable sample/control pair is available to validate Δρ itself**.
**Write "exchangeable", never "matched".** An earlier draft of this section said matched pairs "exist by
construction" in simulation; they do not — `harness.jl:124-136` draws two independent priors, so nuisances
differ between the simulated sample and control just as they do between two specimens. This is a
write-once document, so the wrong mechanism would be permanent and citable. A specimen contrast between the anchors may appear as an illustrative figure, labelled a specimen
difference and explicitly not the scored Δρ. This milestone already carries several named limits and they
have cost it nothing; a quiet pairing of the two anchors would cost it a great deal.

Reporting the two single-stack maps here is therefore **not** the "different quantities" defect: all three
maps are first-class deliverables *everywhere*, the SC3 criterion is single-stack on both arms, and the one
quantity that genuinely cannot transfer is named as not transferring.
