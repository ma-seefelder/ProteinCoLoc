## BLOCKER — DO NOT EXECUTE THIS PLAN UNTIL THE Δρ SEMANTICS ARE SETTLED

This plan writes a **write-once, frozen** document, so a semantic question that belongs in it cannot be
fixed later. The plan-checker gate raised one, and it is unresolved as of this plan's commit:

**In this milestone `Δρ` means sample minus control, and no Phase-12 plan builds a contrast.**
`src/amortized/infer.jl:107` defines `delta_rho(est, Zsample, Zcontrol, θzt)` as "the Monte-Carlo
difference of TWO INDEPENDENT single-stack passes (D-03)". `src/amortized/local_map.jl:48` documents
`LOCAL_MAP_SENTINEL = 0.0` as "no evidence of a **sample-vs-control** difference", and `:30` names
`SpatialColocResult` / `delta_rho_map` as the calibrated successor of exactly that map.
`src/results.jl:181` types the field as `region_delta_rho :: Matrix{Float64}  # per-region Δρ lattice`.

But Phase 12's θ layout is `c₀` + 63 DCT deviations + 7 nuisances + `r₁` — no Δρ row. The simulator draws
ONE `rho_field` per dataset, the pool stores one, and `p12_coloc_map` takes one `mci` and reads ρ units.
Phase 12's own CONTEXT and RESEARCH never mention a control image at all, so this is an inherited gap
between the phase's framing and the package's vocabulary rather than anyone's oversight. Nothing catches
it either: 12-12's range guard admits `[-2, 2]`, so a ρ field in `[-1, 1]` passes silently — the one check
that could catch the mislabelling is what hides it.

**Two resolutions, and this document must encode whichever is chosen:**
- **(a) Deliver true Δρ.** `p12_coloc_map` takes `(bundle, mci, control_mci)` and forms the per-region MC
  difference mirroring `infer.jl:108`; the pool stores a paired second field draw so 12-16's
  parameter-coverage cross-check and 12-18's SBC have a per-region Δρ* truth. Costs a new task in 12-09,
  changes in 12-16 and 12-18, and roughly doubles 12-19's forward passes.
- **(b) Redefine Δρ for this phase** as the per-region deviation from the global term — which is exactly
  what DCT rows 2..64 already are, and a fair reading of D-01's "global ρ + low-rank per-region deviation
  field". Cheap, but it is not what `delta_rho` means anywhere else in the package, and it would leave two
  incompatible senses of one word.

**Recommendation on record: (a)**, because `local_map.jl:30` is an explicit promise that this phase
supersedes a sample-vs-control map, and 12-12 already copies that map's sentinel semantics verbatim.

**Costed in advance, in case the answer is a third thing — "ship the sample map, the control map AND the
difference".** This is *nearly free on top of (a)* and the reason is worth stating, because the intuition
that it triples the work is wrong: a paired Δρ computation already performs **both** single-stack reads
internally (`infer.jl:108` is `mean(ρ_sample_draws .- ρ_control_draws)`, so both draw sets exist before the
subtraction). Reporting all three therefore costs **no additional forward passes** — the marginal cost is
artifact keys and storage, not compute:
- three per-region matrices instead of one (`region_rho_sample`, `region_rho_control`,
  `region_delta_rho`) plus their `region_sd` counterparts, in `spike/p12/result.jl` and every artifact
  that carries a map — roughly 6 × 64 Float64 per dataset rather than 2 × 64;
- 12-16 and 12-19 each gain two reported arms in their tables (the two single-stack maps beside the
  difference), which is table width, not runtime;
- 12-18 gains a decision rather than a cost: whether SBC ranks the difference only, or also the two
  single-stack terms. Ranking all three widens the row-class partition and interacts with `n_low`, so it
  should be settled at the same time as item 3 of `12-CONSTANTS-FOR-CONFIRMATION.md`;
- 12-12's `[-2, 2]` range guard must then apply **only** to the difference, with `[-1, 1]` on the two
  single-stack maps — which is strictly better than today, since it is the guard's current uniform `[-2,2]`
  that lets a mislabelled ρ map pass unnoticed.

**No plan has been restructured for this.** It is recorded so the option can be chosen without a fresh
costing round; option (a) remains the recommendation, and (c) is (a) plus reporting breadth.

Under **(a)** add a §5 to the amendment specifying the paired construction and the control-image
requirement. Under **(b)** add a §5 declaring the redefinition explicitly, and 12-12 must rename the field
or document the divergence, with the `[-2, 2]` bound comment corrected. **Either way the amendment says
which.** Do not execute this plan while the question is open.
