the patch-correlation summary.

prior edge it moves the summary by ‖Δs‖₂ = 1.2417 against a floor of 0.9765 (ratio 1.272), roughly 3×

vacuous, the confound is live and the guards in 12-20 carry the weight. Either answer is publishable;
not measuring it is not.

</objective>
