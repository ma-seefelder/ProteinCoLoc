<objective>
Score the leave-region-out predictive comparison on genuine microscopy data, which is the only part of
SC3 that a simulation cannot supply, and state the resulting claim at exactly the strength the data
support.

Purpose: D-09 made the coverage criterion ground-truth-free precisely so it could run on real images, and
D-11 (AMENDED) settled which images. The temptation this plan exists to resist is overstating the result:
the six committed TIFFs are three channels each of **two** images, so the honest n is 2 images × ~64
regions, and the effective sample is *regions*, which are not independent — they share every nuisance and
the global term within an image. A per-region coverage number computed over 128 correlated regions and
reported as though it were 128 independent trials would be the single most inviting overclaim in the
phase.
Output: the `--real` arm of the existing runner, one reported artifact, and a decoupling test extended to
prove the sealed holdout stayed sealed.
</objective>

**THE SEALED HOLDOUT IS THE ONE IRREVERSIBLE MISTAKE AVAILABLE IN THIS PHASE.** `corpus/manifest.csv`'s
two `physical-primary` rows are `split = sealed_holdout`, `sha256 = PENDING-FETCH`, `bytes = 0`, reserved
for the Phase-16 blind evaluation. D-11's amendment records that the first version of that decision chose
them, and that reading their `split` column is what caught it. Scoring them here would irreversibly burn
Phase 16 — there is no undo, because the value of a blind holdout is destroyed by being looked at once.
The 30 CBS Red-Green rows are separately excluded: they are `simulated-secondary` (computer-generated), so
they cannot support a "real image" claim regardless of fetch status. **This plan reads
`test/test_images/` and nothing else**, and the assertion is executable, not documentary.

**THE DATA, COUNTED HONESTLY.** `test/test_images/positive/positive_c{1,2,3}.tif` and
`test/test_images/negative/negative_c{1,2,3}.tif` are 1028×1376 and already exercised by
`test/runtests.jl`. Those are three channels of a positive image and three of a negative one — **two
images**, not six. At G = 8 each contributes ~64 regions, minus any region `correlation()` cannot score.
The frozen amendment §4(f) already commits the phase to stating "2 images × 64 regions, so the effective
sample is REGIONS, not images", and to reporting the realized per-region n rather than the nominal 128.

**REAL DATA IS WHERE OOD STOPS BEING THEORETICAL.** Everything before this plan ran on the simulator's own
draws, so the summary was in-distribution by construction. These images were not generated by the forward
model. A region that is out of distribution produces a posterior the coverage number has no right to
count, so OOD screening is a precondition of scoring here rather than a diagnostic beside it. Follow the
`local_map.jl` discipline: an unscorable region carries the sentinel **and** a forced OOD flag, and a
missing operating point reads as NOT CHECKED rather than in-distribution.
