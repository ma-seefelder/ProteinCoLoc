<objective>
Prove, at the pre-registered sample size and against the tolerance frozen before the field simulator
existed, that D-05's copula leaves **every one of the 64 regions** with exactly the `MU_PRIOR` marginal.

Purpose: SIM-02 is the CLAUDE.md constraint that keeps π(θ) comparable with the Turing μ-prior and hence
with the ADVI baseline. `prior.jl:42-46` established the repo's discipline for a claim of this kind — a
W1 tolerance declared *before* the calibration run, never tuned to pass. Phase 12 makes the same claim
64 times over, and R-8 established that it is **false at the boundary** without the per-cell rescale. This
plan is where that stops being a screening test and becomes the phase's committed evidence.
Output: `run_p12_sim02.jl`, its reported artifact, and one testset that holds the artifact to the bar.
</objective>

**Two numbers must not be confused.** `P12_SIM02_W1_TOL_PERREGION = 0.10` is the **pre-registered bar**,
identical to `prior.jl`'s `SIM02_W1_TOL` and fixed in Tier 1 before any Phase-12 field existed. The
research's measured **0.00732** is a *forecast* of what this run will find. The bar is not derived from the
forecast, and the summary must say so in as many words — reverse-engineering a tolerance from a result is
the failure mode Tier 1 exists to prevent.

**Gated vs reported inside one artifact.** The W1 max **gates** (`P12_SIM02_W1_TOL_PERREGION` is a member
of `P12_GATING_CONSTANTS`). The atom mass is **reported only** (`P12_VACUOUS_SHRINKAGE_FLOOR`'s neighbour in
`P12_REPORTING_ONLY_CONSTANTS`), and the caption must say so, because recording it as a gate is precisely
how the Phase-7 over-powered-χ² artifact was manufactured.

This runner needs no trained network and no image simulation — it exercises the **prior only**, which is
why it is affordable at M = 20 000.
