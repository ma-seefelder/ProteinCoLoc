<objective>
Answer the question the whole phase rests on — *do the other 63 regions tell you anything about the one
you held out?* — with a closed-form linear probe, before a single network is trained.

Purpose: D-12 Stage 1 exists because a single post-training gate would place the descope decision after
the phase's largest cost is sunk, which is the moment an honest no-go is hardest to call. Phase 11 proved
this exact instrument works and proved what it costs to skip it: its λ marginals widened 4.84–9.05× and
were read as the conditioning being "alive and used" when 12.0 was the prior-echo ceiling reachable by a
network ignoring the image entirely. A ridge is the right tool here precisely because it is weak and
transparent — closed-form, no tuning theatre, no capacity to memorise.
Output: `run_p12_stage1_ridge.jl`, its artifact, and a committed verdict.
</objective>

**This is the ONE Phase-12 runner whose header must not say "gates nothing".** Every other reported runner
in this phase is a diagnostic. This one is `P12_GATING_CONSTANTS` in action: if it fails,
the phase descopes to D-13 **before** the ~1.8 h mini-spike and the ~2.5 h full iteration are spent.

**The three load-bearing features of the Phase-11 template, preserved exactly** (12-RESEARCH § Code
Examples):
1. the predictor set **EXCLUDES** the row that would answer the question trivially — here, region r's own
   continuous row **and** its mask row, zeroed exactly the way `encode_d01` encodes an unusable patch;
2. the baseline is *predict the conditional prior mean*, computed **EMPIRICALLY** from the realized draws
   in each rung, so the comparison cannot be flattered by an analytic mismatch;
3. a **positive control** proves the harness is live — the same ridge with row r **included**, which must
   land far below 1.0, or the null means only that the script is wrong.

**The target is the GAUSSIAN field value** `z_field[r]` (R-2: the sampled parameter, atom-free). The ρ-space
target `rho_field[r]` is run as a **secondary reported arm**, because its ratio is contaminated by the
elementwise `ghat` clamp — a 6.79 %-per-region atom mass compresses the target's variance and flatters any
predictor. The gate is on the Gaussian arm. Say this in the artifact caption; do not let a reader assume the
gate was on ρ.

**What Pitfall 2 predicts, so the result can be read rather than reacted to.** Under the copula every
region's marginal is `MU_PRIoR` regardless of r₁, so the **within-image contrast is controlled entirely by
the correlation length**: short length → large contrast, easily recoverable; long length → the field is
nearly constant, so there is nothing per-region to recover *and nothing to lose*. A ladder result that
improves monotonically with r₁ and crosses the measured per-draw noise floor (**0.0662** in raw correlation
units) somewhere in the middle is the expected shape, and the crossing point is a genuine scientific
deliverable, not an inconvenience. This is why `P12_STAGE1_MIN_RUNGS = 1`: the gate asks whether spatial
borrowing exists **anywhere** in the prior's support, not everywhere.
