<objective>
Produce the two trained networks the rest of the phase scores: the spatial model on the prior 12-15
selected, and its D-10 matched ablation.

Purpose (D-10, R-4): every claim in SC3 is a *difference* between these two nets, so the difference must
be attributable to the spatial prior and to nothing else. R-4 is explicit that the ablation **retrains on
an r₁ → 0 prior** rather than pinning r₁ at read time, because a read-time pin is an out-of-distribution
read and would confound the very gate D-10 exists to make attributable. This plan is where that
discipline is either honoured or quietly lost, so it holds every other knob — architecture, pool size,
image-size mixture, epochs, batch size, masking distribution, split, standardizer arm, seed counter —
literally identical across the two arms, and asserts that it did.
Output: two bundles, one reported artifact recording their content hashes.
</objective>
