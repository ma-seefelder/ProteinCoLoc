<objective>
Test the explanation that would make this phase's headline result meaningless, then record the D-12
Stage-2 verdict.

Purpose (S-4): `CHROMATIC_PRIOR = Uniform(-0.02, 0.02)` applies a **radial magnification difference**
between the two channels, so the effective per-patch correlation varies **radially in every training
draw**, with no biological field present. Measured: holding everything fixed except `chromatic_eps`
0 → 0.02 moves the summary by ‖Δ‖₂ = 0.72–2.59 against an independent-draw noise floor of 0.9765, with
`cor(|Δ|, radius) = +0.48 to +0.83` and a linear-in-radius model explaining 18–69 % of the perturbation —
roughly 3× larger than the maximal registration shift. A spatial map can therefore score well on D-09's
leave-region-out coverage by learning that radial signature and never touching biology. This is not a
hypothetical: it is the same failure class as D-06's grid-alignment artifact, and it was surfaced by the
premise audit specifically because a naive map would report it as a genuine spatial finding.
Output: four guard measurements and the phase's Stage-2 verdict.
</objective>
