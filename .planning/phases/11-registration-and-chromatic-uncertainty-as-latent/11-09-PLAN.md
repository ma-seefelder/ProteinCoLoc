The pre-registered breakdown definition (RESEARCH §E18), already implemented as
`breakdown_point(ks, n, rungs)` in `spike/validation/p11_stats.jl`:
> the smallest rung magnitude `x` on the pre-registered beyond-prior ladder at which the **upper
> limb** of the 90 percent two-sided Wilson interval for empirical 90 percent-CI coverage falls
> strictly below nominal 0.90. Report as "coverage holds to below `x`; degrades at and beyond `x`".
> If no rung satisfies it, report right-censored. The upper limb rather than the point estimate
> makes "coverage has genuinely dropped" an evidentiary claim; "first" rather than "last" is
> unambiguous under non-monotonicity.

Beyond-prior ladders, from `p11_consts.jl` (Tier-1):
`SC3_SHIFT_RUNGS = (3.5, 4.0, 5.0, 6.0, 8.0)` px along the diagonal (`dx = dy = x/sqrt(2)`) with
`chromatic_eps` from its prior; `SC3_EPS_RUNGS = (0.025, 0.03, 0.04, 0.05)` with
`shift ~ Uniform(-3, 3)`; `N_PER_RUNG = 500` each; read at `SC3_READ_LAMBDA_PRIMARY = 3.0` and
`SC3_READ_LAMBDA_SECONDARY = 1.0`.

The D-02 attenuation protocol (RESEARCH risk R6, lines 1809-1832):
1. Generate ONE evaluation set of `K = 500` (theta*, image) pairs from the **shipped** prior
   (`SHIFT_PRIOR = Uniform(-1, 1)`, `chromatic_eps = 0`) on a fresh `p11_rng` counter — one set,
   used by both nets, so the data is identical.
2. Read it through the **frozen shipped 8x8 net** (`estimator_for(8)`, 128-row input) and through
   the **Phase-11 research net** at `lambda = 1.0` (the level matching the shipped prior).
3. Report `RMSE(rho_hat, rho*)` for each, `mean(post_sd)` for each — **this is the attenuation** —
   and 90 percent coverage for each.
4. `attenuation = mean(post_sd_research) / mean(post_sd_frozen)`. A value near 1.0 at lambda = 1.0
   is the good outcome. A value well above 1.0 means the widening leaked into the low-lambda regime
   and must be reported as a cost.
