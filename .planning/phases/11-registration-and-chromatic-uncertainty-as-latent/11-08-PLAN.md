<objective>
Run the SC2 ladder: read the Delta-rho posterior at each rung of the registration-uncertainty
ladder and test both parts of D-07 — that the posterior widens monotonically, and that its coverage
stays honestly at nominal while it widens.

Purpose: part (b) is what makes the widening *honest* rather than merely larger. It rules out a net
that inflates variance without tracking truth, which is what the ROADMAP goal ("widens honestly
instead of reporting false confidence") actually asserts. This same run also satisfies SC3's
in-prior coverage claim — D-07(b) and SC3-in-prior are literally the same statistic on the same
ladder, and running them twice would be waste dressed as rigour.

Output: `spike/validation/p11_harness.jl`, `spike/validation/run_p11_ladder.jl` and the gated
ladder report.
</objective>

**Binding orchestrator ruling carried here — Q3.** SC2's monotone-widening claim is about

contributes a constant marginal spread at every rung; its dose-response is measured only by the

this scope boundary explicitly so no reader takes SC2 as covering chromatic misalignment.

**Binding orchestrator ruling carried here — Q7.** The ladder evaluates on the **same F5 image-size
mixture** the net trained on. Train-joint == eval-joint is F5's binding invariant.

**The most dangerous single line in this plan** is the Holm comparison. `sbc_holm_pass` is
`all(>(fwer), ...)` — correct for a uniformity null where rejection is bad. The equivalence family
needs the **inverted** direction: `all(<=(fwer), p11_holm_adjusted(p_tost))`, because here
rejection is good. Use `p11_tost_pass` (plan 11-01) and never `sbc_holm_pass`.

**Over-power does not apply here.** The Phase-7 lesson that M = 2000 point-null KS resolves a
0.059-SD drift and therefore rejects on drift too small to matter does **not** cap N for an
equivalence test: larger N makes equivalence *easier* to establish, because the interval shrinks
toward the point estimate. There is no over-power pathology for TOST, only a cost ceiling. The
report must say this in one sentence, because a reader who remembers "M = 2000 was too big" will
otherwise object to N = 500 per rung.
