<objective>
Measure the realized marginal coverage of the hand-rolled split-conformal hedge, and produce the
shared evaluation pool that three later runners consume.

**Pre-registered band (SC1-d, frozen in `spike/p14/consts.jl` before this runner existed):**
calibrate on `n_cal = P14_N_CAL = 2000` at `P14_CAL_COUNTER`; measure on `n_eval = P14_N_EVAL = 2000`
at `P14_EVAL_COUNTER`; **both unstratified**. Coverage must satisfy
`coverage ≥ 1 − α_conf − 3·√(α(1−α)/n_eval)`, which at `α_conf = 0.10` and `n = 2000` is
**≥ 0.880**. Falsified if realized coverage falls below the band.

Output: `spike/p14/run_p14_conformal.jl`, and the two `.jld2` artifacts it persists.

Compute budget: ≈ 4 min per 2,000-item unstratified set (the 4,000-item gate cost ~11 min WITH a
1.58× stratification overhead an unstratified draw avoids entirely). Nothing is trained.
</objective>
