<objective>
Run the decision layer on the only real microscopy this project may touch, and record — with evidence
— why the corpus check D-03 originally specified is not the check that runs.

**D-03a, which supersedes D-03's corpus half.** The corpus contains **no unsealed physical ground
truth**: 30 of 32 rows are `tier: simulated-secondary` / `truth_label: simulated-degree` (the CBS
benchmark — i.e. another simulator), and the only 2 `physical-primary` rows are Phase 16's
`sealed_holdout`. Its bytes are also unfetched by design (`.gitignore:451-453` keeps downloaded image
bytes out of git; `corpus/fetch.jl` is the fetcher). So a corpus check would be neither physical nor
runnable. Phase 14 therefore uses the **same six committed TIFFs Phase 13 sanctioned**
(`spike/p13/real_images.jl:71,145` — *"THE SANCTIONED SUBSTITUTE IS THE SIX COMMITTED MICROSCOPY
TIFFS UNDER test/test_images/"*, adopted *"precisely so that Phase 16's blind corpus stays blind"*).

**Two things this plan must NOT do.** It must not emit a coverage number from six images in two
conditions — that bounds nothing tightly and must be reported as an **illustration**. And the
withdrawn figure `α ≥ 1/31 ≈ 0.032` must appear nowhere: it was computed from the wrong n and on rows
that are not physical truth.

**A prediction, recorded as a prediction and not an input (Assumptions Log A6):** 13-REPORT limit D
records both fixtures scoring density 703.30 against their own threshold 167.54 — 4.198× — so under
D-05 the correct output for both is **ABSTAIN**. That is a result worth reporting: *on the only real
microscopy this project holds, the decision layer abstains, and that is the designed behaviour.* If
they do NOT abstain despite those scores, that is a finding about the OOD wiring worth chasing.

Output: `spike/p14/run_p14_real_images.jl`, `spike/p14/p14_real_images_report.jld2`.
</objective>
