<objective>
Measure SC3-d: that abstention concentrates on OOD cases.

**SC3-d (GATED):** `abstain_rate(misspecified at the strongest level) − abstain_rate(in-distribution)
≥ P14_OOD_MARGIN_FLOOR = 0.50` (absolute), on matched `n = P14_N_OOD = 1000` per arm at
`P14_OOD_COUNTER`. Falsified if abstention is no more frequent under a strong, deliberately injected
misspecification than in-distribution.

This is a **different claim from SC3-c** and both are needed, because SC3's *"hard/OOD"* is a slash,
not a synonym. SC3-c asks whether abstention tracks *would-have-been-wrong*; SC3-d asks whether it
tracks *the data is not what the model was trained on*.

`P14_OOD_MARGIN_FLOOR = 0.50` is a **JUDGEMENT CALL WITH NO DERIVATION**, frozen in 14-01 before this
runner existed, and must be labelled as such in the artifact and the report.

Output: `spike/p14/run_p14_ood_arm.jl` and `spike/p14/p14_ood_arm_report.jld2`.
</objective>
