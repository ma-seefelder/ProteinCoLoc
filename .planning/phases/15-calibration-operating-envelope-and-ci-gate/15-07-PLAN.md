Spend the phase's compute once, on the shipped net, on a fresh stream, and write down what it
measured.

Purpose: this is the only compute-bearing step. The budget is ~8.5 h at 16+ threads and the sweep
must not be re-run to check a code change — every mechanism it uses was already proven at fixture
scale in plans 15-03, 15-04 and 15-05. The anchor is measured FIRST, at the sweep's own M on the
Phase-15 stream, because that is what makes the ECE noise floor common-mode and removes the D-04a
failure mode; and it is appended as a Tier-2 constant so the threshold that will judge every rung is
on the record before any rung is judged.

Output: the Tier-2 anchor block, `test/gate/run_p15_envelope.jl`, and
`artifacts/amended_v2/grid_8/envelope_report_8.jld2`.
