The generator contract every family satisfies (misspec.jl:104-105, 125-126, 150-151, 166-167):
  gen(rng, θ; imsize = SBC_IMSIZE, level::Integer = OOD_GRID_LEVELS, G::Integer = 8)
      -> Vector{Matrix{Float64}}   # exactly 2 elements, each `imsize`, all finite and >= 0
  - `rng` positional and untyped (a Philox4x in practice); `θ` positional and untyped (the 8-field
    NamedTuple from `ProteinCoLoc.sample_prior`)
  - all three of imsize/level/G are keywords and ALL THREE are always supplied by the consumer
    (misspec.jl:371 — `img = gen(rng, θ; imsize = isz, level = lvl, G = G)`), so the declared
    defaults are decorative at the call site but must exist for signature compatibility
  - every family opens with `_guard_misspec_imsize(imsize, G)` (misspec.jl:76-83): throws unless both
    dims >= 64 AND `imsize[d] ÷ G >= 4`
  - the return is `[ch1, ch2]`, `Matrix{Float64}`, clamped with `max.(·, 0.0)`

How the family table is consumed (misspec.jl:316-396, run_gate.jl:290-295): `gate_ood_roc` takes
`families = OOD_FAMILIES` as a KEYWORD and uses only `keys(families)` and `families[fam]`; it loops
`for lvl in 1:levels` with `levels` also a keyword. `ood_gate` forwards both. A new family therefore
slots in by passing a DIFFERENT NamedTuple — never by editing the frozen one.

`misspec_simulator(gen; level, G)` (misspec.jl:194-199) already wraps a generator into the exact
`(sample_prior, simulate_pair, build_mci)` contract `sbc_gate` consumes as `sim`, holding the prior
at π(θ) — the misspecification is in the FORWARD MODEL, which is what model misspecification means.

Simulator facts that bound the ladders (src/amortized/simulator.jl):
  - `simulate_pair` validates `0.0 <= spillover <= 1.0` (:233) and `autofluorescence >= 0` (:235);
    there is NO range guard on `shift_dx`/`shift_dy` beyond finiteness
  - stages 4 (spillover mix) and 6 (sub-pixel shift) consume NO rng, and the autofluorescence offset
    is added rather than sampled — so overriding those θ fields leaves rng consumption unchanged
  - large shifts fill the vacated border with `ProteinCoLoc.BG_FLOOR` (:290), which is itself part of
    the misspecification and must be named as such in the docstring
  - `θ` is a NamedTuple, so `merge(θ, (; spillover = s))` is the override idiom

Ladders frozen in test/gate/p15_consts.jl by plan 15-01:
  P15_SPILLOVER_LADDER = (0.1, 0.2, 0.35, 0.6, 1.0)      rung 2 == maximum(SPILLOVER_PRIOR)
  P15_SHIFT_LADDER     = (0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0, 8.0)  px   rung 2 == maximum(SHIFT_PRIOR)
  P15_AF_LADDER        = (0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8)      rung 2 == maximum(AUTOFLUORESCENCE_PRIOR)
  P15_AXIS_MECHANISM   = 7 axes typed :in_prior | :out_of_model  (background is :out_of_model)
