The ECE identity, derived from sbc.jl:302-315 + :60-108 and verified numerically against the shipped
report to the last float digit:
  ECE = mean over α in 0.05:0.05:0.95 of |α − ĉ(α)|, where
  ĉ(α) = mean(abs.((ranks .+ 0.5) ./ (L + 1) .- 0.5) .<= α / 2)
It does not depend on `SBC_BINS` at all, because with 19 α values in 50 equal-width bins each α lands
in its own bin, 31 bins are empty, and empty bins carry weight 0 in the ECE sum. `mce`, by contrast,
walks ALL 50 bins including the empty ones, where `predicted_rate = midpoint` and
`observed_rate = 0.0` — which is why every one of the eight columns of the shipped report records
`mce = 0.99`.
