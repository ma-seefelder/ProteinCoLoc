<objective>
Build and train the Phase-4 `PosteriorEstimator` — an MLP summary net feeding a NeuralEstimators
`NormalisingFlow` over the 7-dim simulator θ (ρ_true is row 1) — on the Phase-3 leak-free fixed-data
loader tensors, CPU-only, and provide the inference surface that returns single-pass posteriors,
ρ̂ = row-1 posterior mean, the Δρ Monte-Carlo difference (D-03), interval widths, and the μ→ρ ghat
mapping the benchmark needs. Persist the trained estimator and turn SC1 green.

(04-06) consume this trained estimator and these inference wrappers.
